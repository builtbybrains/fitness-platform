About us

Health should be simpler than this.

We believe achieving a healthier lifestyle should be simpler, more personal and easier to maintain.

Our mission

Turn intention into a routine that survives a busy week.

Our goal is to combine artificial intelligence, nutrition guidance and personal training into one simple daily experience.

Most people do not fail because they lack discipline. They fail because the plan was never theirs, the advice contradicted itself, and staying on track required more attention than a normal life allows.

VITAL is designed to help people build sustainable habits rather than rely on temporary motivation. The plan adapts to your body, your schedule and your preferences, and it keeps showing up quietly, every day.

What we believe

Four principles behind every decision we make.

01
Personal by defaultA plan that is not built around your body, goal and preferences is just advice. We start from you and never ship a generic template.
02
Quiet, not noisyNotifications should reduce effort, not add to it. If a reminder is not useful at that exact moment, it should not exist.
03
Honest pacingSustainable beats dramatic. We would rather you lose seven kilos and keep them off than fifteen and rebound.
04
One place for everythingNutrition and training are one system. Splitting them across apps is how consistency dies.
