Good to know.

Is there a free trial?

No. There is no free trial. BUILT is $30 a month, and you can cancel anytime.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from inside the app in a couple of taps. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Why only one plan?

No. New features are included in the same subscription.

What payment methods do you accept?

More Than Fitness.

We believe a healthier life should be simpler, more personal and easier to keep up.

Most people do not fail because they lack discipline. They fail because the plan was never theirs, the advice contradicted itself, and staying on track took more attention than a normal life allows.

BUILT is designed to help you build habits that last rather than rely on motivation that fades. The plan adapts to your body, your schedule and your preferences, and it keeps showing up, quietly, every day. Nutrition, training and tracking live in one app, and it takes about two minutes to go from signing up to your first full plan.

Four principles behind every decision we make.

Personal by defaultA plan that is not built around your body, goal and preferences is just advice. We start from you and never ship a generic template.
Quiet, not noisyNotifications should reduce effort, not add to it. If a reminder is not useful at that exact moment, it should not exist.
Honest pacingSustainable beats dramatic. We would rather you lose seven kilos and keep them off than fifteen and rebound.
One place for everythingNutrition and training are one system. Splitting them across apps is how consistency dies.

Privacy and terms.

Last updated: October 2026.

Privacy Policy: what we collect

To build your plan we collect what you tell us during setup: your name, phone number, date of birth, gender, height and weight, activity level, goal and timeline, where and when you train, food preferences and allergies, and any injuries or medical conditions you choose to share. We also keep what you log day to day, such as workouts, meals and food photos, activities, weigh-ins, check-ins, body photos, your messages to the coach and any problems you report. If you turn on Apple Health or Health Connect, we read only the steps, active calories, workouts, weight and sleep you allow.

Privacy Policy: body photos

You take a body photo at sign-up and again at your monthly check-ins. Your face is blurred on your phone before the photo is uploaded, so an unblurred photo never leaves your device. The photos are stored privately, where only your account can open them. The AI uses them to estimate your starting point and suggest the training approach that suits you. Our support and admin tools never show them.

Privacy Policy: how we use it

Your information is used to generate and adapt your nutrition and training recommendations, to send the reminders you have turned on, to answer the problems you report, and to show you your own progress. Your phone number and email are kept on file so we can reach you about your account and invite you to BUILT events. We never sell your phone number or any other personal information, share your health data with advertisers, or use your data to build profiles for third parties.

Privacy Policy: services that handle your data

Your account and the data you log are stored with Supabase, the database and sign-in service BUILT runs on.

When you send a food photo, a body photo or a message to the coach, it is sent to an outside AI provider to produce the estimate, the suggestion or the reply. Today that provider is OpenRouter, and BUILT uses its free AI models. Free models may keep what they are sent and use it to improve their systems, so leave out anything you would not want stored. Estimates are approximate, so treat them as a guide.

Privacy Policy: security and your rights

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited to the people who need it to run and support the service. You can ask us to delete your account data at any time by writing to us through the contact form with the topic “Privacy or data”. Deleting removes your personal data from our production systems, with backups aging out on a rolling schedule.

Terms of Service: using BUILT

BUILT provides personalized nutrition and fitness guidance generated by software. You must be at least 13 years old to use it. If you are 13 to 17, you need the consent of a parent or guardian, which the app asks for at sign-up along with their name; plans for under-18s never set a calorie deficit, give supplement advice or prescribe max-effort lifting. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.

BUILT is a wellness product, not a medical service, and its recommendations do not replace advice from a doctor, dietitian or other qualified professional. Everyone accepts a short waiver at sign-up: check with a doctor before starting if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, and you train at your own risk.

Terms of Service: billing and acceptable use

Access is provided through a $30 monthly subscription, billed through the App Store or Google Play, and renews automatically until cancelled; you can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of the paid period. Do not attempt to disrupt the service, reverse engineer it, or use it to distribute unlawful or harmful content. If a change materially reduces what you have paid for, we will tell you in advance. Questions about these terms: use the contact form.
