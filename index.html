Questions

Good to know.

Is there a free trial?

You can explore the app and complete setup before subscribing, so you see your generated plan before you pay for it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from inside the app in a couple of taps. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Why only one plan?

Because tiers force you to guess which version of a product you need. Nutrition and training work together, so splitting them into packages would make the product worse.

Do you charge for updates?

No. New features are included in the same subscription.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment is handled through the App Store and Google Play, so any method already on your account works.

About us

Built for people, not for streak charts.

We believe achieving a healthier lifestyle should be simpler, more personal and easier to maintain.

Most people do not fail because they lack discipline. They fail because the plan was never theirs, the advice contradicted itself, and staying on track required more attention than a normal life allows.

What we believe

Four principles behind every decision we make.

01

Privacy & Terms.

Last updated: January 2026.

Privacy Policy — what we collect

To build your plan we collect the information you provide during setup, such as weight, height, age, activity level, goals and food preferences, along with what you log day to day, such as meals, workouts, water and progress measurements.

Privacy Policy — how we use it

Your information is used to generate and adapt your nutrition and training recommendations, to send the reminders you have enabled, and to show you your own progress. We also use aggregated, non-identifying usage data to improve the product. We do not sell your personal information, share your health data with advertisers, or use your data to build profiles for third parties.

Privacy Policy — storage, security & your rights

Data is stored encrypted in transit and at rest, with internal access limited to the people who need it to operate and support the service. You can export your data or delete your account at any time from inside the app; deleting your account removes your personal data from our production systems, with backups aging out on a rolling schedule. Questions: privacy@vital.app.

Terms of Service — using VITAL

VITAL provides personalized nutrition and fitness guidance generated by software. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account, and you are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure. VITAL is a wellness product, not a medical service, and its recommendations do not replace advice from a doctor, dietitian or other qualified professional.

Terms of Service — billing & acceptable use

Access is provided through a $30 monthly subscription, billed through the App Store or Google Play, and renews automatically until cancelled; you can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of the paid period. Do not attempt to disrupt the service, reverse engineer it, or use it to distribute unlawful or harmful content. If a change materially reduces what you have paid for, we will tell you in advance. Questions: legal@vital.app.
