Billing questions

Simple, on purpose.

Is there a free trial?

You can explore the app and complete setup before subscribing, so you see your generated plan before you pay for it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from inside the app in a couple of taps. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Why only one plan?

Because tiers force you to guess which version of a product you need. Nutrition and training work together — splitting them into packages would make the product worse.

Do you charge for updates?

No. New features are included in the same subscription.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment is handled through the App Store and Google Play, so any method already on your account works.
