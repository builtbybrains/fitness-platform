How it works

Your Plan. Your Goals. Powered by AI.

Four steps from “I should probably start” to a plan you actually follow, and a system that keeps adapting as you go.

01
Step 01

Tell Us About You

Setup takes about two minutes. We ask only for what genuinely changes your plan, and nothing else.

  • Current weight and height
  • Age and activity level
  • Your goal and a realistic timeline
  • Food preferences, allergies and dislikes
  • How many days a week you can train
02
Step 02

AI Builds Your Plan

Your calorie and macro targets, meal recommendations and weekly training structure are generated together, as one plan.

  • Daily calorie and macro targets
  • Meal recommendations for every slot
  • A weekly training split that fits your schedule
  • Water and step targets
  • A pace that is sustainable, not extreme
03
Step 03

Follow Your Daily Plan

Open the app and the day is already laid out. Meals, workout and targets, with no decisions to make before you have had coffee.

  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks
  • Today’s workout with sets and reps
  • Hydration and activity nudges
  • One-tap logging
  • Reminders at the moments that matter
04
Step 04

Track. Improve. Repeat.

Everything you log feeds back into the plan. As your body and habits change, so do the recommendations.

  • Weight and measurements over time
  • Streaks and consistency scoring
  • A weekly review of what worked
  • Automatic plan adjustments
  • Goals that move with you

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. Your plan is built around what you have access to, whether that is a full gym, a few dumbbells at home, or nothing but bodyweight. You tell us during setup and the workouts are generated accordingly.

How does the AI actually personalize my plan?

Your targets start from your body composition, goal and activity level. From there, everything you log feeds back in, including meals, workouts, weight and consistency, so the plan stays realistic instead of drifting out of date.

What if I have allergies or foods I dislike?

Set them once during setup and recommendations respect them permanently. You can also mark prep time and budget preferences.

Can I change my goal later?

Yes. Change your goal at any point and the nutrition targets and training structure regenerate around the new one.

Will the reminders be annoying?

They are context-aware. If you have already logged lunch, you will not get a lunch reminder. You can also mute any category individually.
